About Us

AquaNex is a simple educational website about modern aquaculture. It provides basic information about fish farming, water quality and pond management.

Our main goal is to make aquaculture information easy to understand for students, farmers and anyone interested in aquatic farming.

Our Project

This project focuses on aquaculture, water quality and simple calculations that can help in basic aquaculture farm management.
