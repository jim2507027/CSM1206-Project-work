Aquaculture

Understanding Modern Aquatic Farming

What is Aquaculture?

Aquaculture is the farming of aquatic organisms such as fish, shrimp, shellfish and aquatic plants under controlled or semi-controlled conditions.

It is an important source of food, employment and income. Modern aquaculture focuses on increasing production while protecting water quality and the environment.

🐟 Fish Farming

Raising fish in ponds, tanks, cages and other suitable culture systems.

🦐 Shrimp Farming

Culturing shrimp in ponds with proper water and feeding management.

🌱 Sustainable Farming

Producing aquatic food while reducing environmental impact and resource waste.

Major Aquaculture Systems

  • Pond culture system
  • Tank culture system
  • Cage culture system
  • Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS)
  • Integrated aquaculture system

Importance of Aquaculture

  • Provides nutritious aquatic food.
  • Creates employment opportunities.
  • Supports rural communities.
  • Reduces pressure on wild fish populations.
  • Contributes to food security.
