Water Quality

Good Water • Healthy Fish • Better Production

Why Water Quality Matters?

Good water quality is very important for fish growth and healthy aquaculture.

Important Water Quality Parameters

💧 pH

pH shows whether water is acidic, neutral or alkaline.

6.5 - 8.5

🌡️ Temperature

Temperature affects fish growth, metabolism and oxygen.

Species dependent

🫧 Dissolved Oxygen

Oxygen in water is necessary for fish respiration.

5 mg/L or more

⚗️ Ammonia

High ammonia can be harmful to fish.

Keep low

🌊 Salinity

Salinity is important for shrimp and marine species.

Species dependent

🔵 Turbidity

Turbidity tells us how clear or cloudy the water is.

Moderate

Water Management Tips

  • Check water quality regularly.
  • Do not overfeed the fish.
  • Remove extra waste.
  • Maintain proper oxygen.
  • Keep proper stocking density.
  • Change or treat water when needed.
